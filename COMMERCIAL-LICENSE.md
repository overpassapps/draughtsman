complete source code, and work you combine with it can fall under the same terms. Under this licence, you may change Draughtsman
and connect it to your own systems for your organisation's internal business, **and keep those changes and integrations
private**. You have no duty to publish them or to give their source to anyone. You also get 12 months of updates under these

Draughtsman, ship it inside a product you distribute, or run it as a service for other organisations. If you want any of those,
write to us about a separate agreement.

rely on it.

**7.4** **Commitment for later Builds:** we will not add to the Software any telemetry, usage reporting, automatic update check,
