## How to agree

There is no bot or sign-up service. You agree once, and it covers all your later Contributions to this repository unless you
tell us otherwise:

1. Open your first pull request.
2. Comment on it with exactly this sentence:

   > I have read the CLA and I agree.

   Alternatively, tick the CLA box in the pull request template, which says the same thing.
3. A maintainer will not merge a pull request from someone who has not agreed.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company, write to **draughtsman@overpass.co.uk** and we will arrange a corporate
agreement.
